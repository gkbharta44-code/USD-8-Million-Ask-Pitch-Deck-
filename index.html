01 · The Problem

Friction in global trade and industrial operations

Logistics cost and delay

Fragmented infrastructure and manual processes add cost and delay to cross-border trade. [Source and figure to be added]

Administrative drag

Customs paperwork, compliance checks, and port demurrage consume time and money at every handoff. [Source and figure to be added]

OT security gaps

Legacy industrial control systems are exposed to remote programming and intrusion, and cyber-physical engineering talent is scarce.

B2B trust gaps

Buyers face counterfeit goods, unverified inventory, and upfront payment risk without independent quality checks.

02 · The Solution

Three software modules and one settlement layer

Delper Aegis · OT Security

An inline industrial firewall. Target certification: IEC 62443 Security Level 4 (not yet achieved). Includes a hardware write-lock option against remote programming.

Verified Trade Ledger

A buyer-first sourcing platform with third-party quality gates and a target 14-day settlement cycle, pending pilot validation.

Customs & Logistics Middleware

Software that automates cross-border declaration data and validation steps. Built on integrations that are in development.

Settlement Token (under evaluation)

A proposed corporate settlement layer. Design, reserve structure, and regulatory treatment are not finalized and require legal review before any use.

08 · Risks

Material risks investors should weigh

Commodity and counterparty

Price swings, buyer default, cargo loss, and supplier fraud can each cause losses.

Execution

Pilot results may not validate fee levels. Integration with ERP and customs systems may take longer and cost more than planned.

Technology and certification

Security certifications, hardware performance, and the stress-test results are not yet proven.

Legal and regulatory

Securities treatment of the round, token regulation, cross-border trade rules, and ESG sourcing rules all apply. Counsel review is required.

Key person and talent

The plan depends on scarce cyber-physical and platform engineering talent.

Loss of capital

Investors may lose some or all of their investment. No return or repayment is guaranteed.
